Use for complete-paper drafting or structural revision. Local edits use action and section guides; audits use their diagnostic protocols.

## Work focused sections

Work in a dependency-aware order using one active section guide. Read the short [prose core](polishing-protocol.md#prose-core-for-drafting-and-revision) and [wording-register.md](wording-register.md) once during complete-paper drafting and reuse them across sections; add guidance only for a distinct need. When drafting from research notes, preserve their scientific content while rewriting source-handling language and proof shorthand for the intended reader.

Check each section's intended understanding against its support, names, scope, paragraphs, citations, and displays. Update the plan only for changed decisions; resolve dependent passages together and reuse settled notes. Finalize the title and abstract after the body establishes its emphasis.

## Reconcile the whole paper

Read the assembled manuscript once as a continuous argument, using the source map rather than repeating all local checks:

- Revisit title, abstract, introduction, results, and ending after structural changes: align contribution, evidence strength, and boundary while retaining supplied hierarchy.
- Reconcile definitions, notation, assumptions, numbers, citations, cross-references, and main/supplement dependencies across sections and captions. Remove repeated setup while keeping distant passages understandable.
- Check whether paragraphs and displays develop connected thoughts or fragment them into tiny lead-ins and follow-ups. Consolidate redundant qualifications while preserving scope where needed. Assess proportions, notation density, and display prominence by explanatory value. Not every result needs the same preamble or ending.
- Check credit at substantive use, including methods, comparators, data, and external interpretations. At consequential method choices, can the reader understand the supported relationship to inherited ideas? Identify missing rationale without inventing it. Reconcile figure/table labels with the statistical objects; captions decode displays and body prose explains their contribution.
- Compile/render the complete editable manuscript when possible. Inspect continuity and emphasis as well as clipping: isolated display lead-ins, excessive space for incidental formulas, headings separated from content, floats interrupting key explanations, overfull lines, readable figure/table text, bibliography, and venue length. Repair exposition before adjusting spacing. Respect submission placement rules rather than forcing immediate floats. Check applicable front/back matter using supplied information.

Repair consequential mismatches; recheck affected dependencies and rerender layout changes. Stop when the scope is coherent or remaining issues need new evidence or author choices. Report material unresolved input or unavailable rendering. Ordinary writing creates no audit harness or extra plan artifacts.
